## About me

I'm a student engineer from Bucharest focused on robotics, electronics and embedded systems.

I founded **HyperLine Robotics**, where I design and build competition robots from the ground up, including custom PCBs, firmware, control systems, mechanical integration and testing.

Most of my work is centered around autonomous robots, high-speed line followers, embedded hardware and experimental engineering projects. I also work on cybersecurity and software projects, and I like keeping my technical work open-source whenever possible.

I enjoy taking an idea from a rough concept to something physical that actually works, then improving it through testing, failures and iteration.
